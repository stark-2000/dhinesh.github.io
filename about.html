About Myself

Hi, I'm Dhinesh! Welcome to my portfolio. I'm an Electrical Engineer with 3+ years of industry experience and 2 patents, specializing in electronic hardware design, embedded systems, and mechatronic system development. I am passionate about designing, developing, and troubleshooting reliable electronic systems that address real-world engineering challenges.

🔍 Professional Journey:

My professional journey began at Solinas Integrity, where I developed electronic control systems, custom PCBs, and embedded hardware for robotic pipeline inspection systems. At Digital Blanket, I expanded my expertise in sensor integration, PCB design, and IoT-based electronics, working with thermal imaging, mmWave radar, and embedded sensing technologies. As a Robotics Research Associate at the National Institutes of Health (NIH), I contributed to laboratory automation systems, integrating embedded electronics, sensors, and robotic hardware. Currently, as a Hardware Engineer at Bioptics Technology, I focus on developing advanced medical imaging devices, working across schematic design, multilayer PCB development, power electronics, LED/laser drivers, camera integration, and hardware validation.

🎓 Educational Background:

I hold a Master's degree in Robotics from the University of Maryland and a Bachelor's degree in Electrical and Electronics Engineering. During my undergraduate studies, I was awarded a gold medal for outstanding research in sustainable energy innovation.

🌐 Professional Expertise:

My technical expertise spans analog/digital circuit design, schematic capture, component selection, PCB layout, power management, embedded systems, sensor interfacing, circuit debugging, hardware validation, and regulatory compliance. My multidisciplinary background in electronics, mechatronics, and robotics enables me to approach complex hardware challenges with a system-level perspective.

🚗 Passion and Interests:

I'm passionate about electronic hardware design, product development, embedded electronics, and intelligent electromechanical systems. Known for my attention to detail, curiosity, and collaborative approach, I enjoy taking engineering concepts from initial design through prototyping, testing, and refinement.
